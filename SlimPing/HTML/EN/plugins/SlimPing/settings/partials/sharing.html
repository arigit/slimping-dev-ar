Sharing Settings
Shares are a personal sharing tool, not a broadcast service.
Each share URL is intended for a small, known audience - friends, family, or collaborators. Shares carry hard limits on concurrent listeners (default 5) and unique recipients (default 20) to prevent unintentional wide redistribution. A share that exceeds these limits is automatically revoked.

Respect copyright. Shares are for content you have the right to distribute. They must not be used to operate an unlicensed internet radio station, to publicly redistribute copyrighted material, or to circumvent access controls on streamed content. As the server operator, you are responsible for how shares are used.

Share tracks, albums and playlists via time-limited, unauthenticated URLs. Recipients stream capped-bitrate MP3 through a virtual player - no SlimPing credentials are needed. Active guardrails:

  • Max [% share_max_listeners %] concurrent listeners per share
  • Auto-revoked after [% share_max_unique_ips %] unique IPs
  • Share and radio audio capped at [% share_max_bitrate %] kbps MP3
  • TTL clamped to [% share_min_ttl %]-[% share_max_ttl %] hours
  • Max [% share_user_cap %] active shares per user
