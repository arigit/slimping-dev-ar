Remote Audio Streams

Lyrion supports remote library tracks, songs and albums linked to third party services (Spotify, TIDAL, Qobuz etc.) where protocol-handler plugins fetch and decode content on demand. Lyrion also supports rich Internet Radio streams. SlimPing throttles and controls requests for remote tracks and URL streams to prevent issues and abuse. This may include limiting requests, returning errors on overuse, and restricting clients that make too many requests in quick succession.

SlimPing delivers remote streams by fetching and proxying them through the LMS server, which allows clients to play them regardless of network restrictions or CORS issues. However, this can increase load on the server and may cause playback issues if the upstream service is slow or unreliable.

Remote streams are ALWAYS delivered to OpenSubsonic clients in MP3 format regardless of their original source format.
