SlimPing is an LMS Plugin that exposes an OpenSubsonic REST API server, allowing any Subsonic-compatible client to browse and play your LMS library.

SlimPing Home Page.

SlimPing is free and open source under the GPLv3 licence and has been built with the help of LLMs and builds on the work of others in the LMS community.
