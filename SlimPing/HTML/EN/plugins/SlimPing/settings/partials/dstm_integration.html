DSTM Integration

SlimPing connects Lyrion's DSTM (Don't Stop The Music) mixers to Subsonic clients. When a client asks for similar tracks (getSonicSimilarTracks, findSonicPath, getSimilarSongs) or an active stream's queue runs low, the DSTM mixer you choose below supplies the next tracks. The mixing level controls how much of this integration is active; the classic genre/tag engine remains available as a fallback.
