System Prerequisites & Streaming Services

SlimPing relies on Lyrion's use of external helper binaries for audio transcoding. When a client requests a format the source file is not already in (e.g. FLAC to MP3, OGG to MP3, or a lower bitrate than the original), LMS chains these helpers together to decode and re-encode the audio on the fly. If a required helper is missing, those conversions will fail silently and the client will receive no audio. Remote tracks and Internet Radio streams are always transcoded and depend on the critical helpers below.
